Questions

Straight answers

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and once the final payment is made, the site and its files are yours. If you ever stop the monthly plan, you keep everything.

What if I need a change later?

Small edits like new hours or menu items are covered by the care plan. Bigger changes are quoted up front, so there are no surprise bills.

How long does it take?

A starter site is usually ready for review within a week of receiving your photos and details. Larger sites take longer, and I’ll give you a date before we start.

Do I need to buy anything else?

Just your domain name, usually about $10 to $20 a year. I’ll walk you through buying it in your own name.
